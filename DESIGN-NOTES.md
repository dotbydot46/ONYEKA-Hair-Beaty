# Design notes

The original black, gold and soft pink branding is extended with warm ivory space so the content remains inviting and readable. Strong serif headings introduce each page; local DM Sans supports navigation, service information and forms. The proposed logo uses an oval O with flowing hair lines and a small gold star.

Research patterns applied:

- Charlotte Mensah: a strong editorial identity with a direct link to the salon location.
- Afrotherapy: clear service-specific navigation and information that supports customer enquiries.
- Hershesons: concise editorial sections and distinct paths to explore the business.

ONYEKA’s content and journeys remain based on the owner’s supplied service list and contact details. No competitor claims, testimonials, prices or service photographs were reused.

The main customer journeys are: explore services → find a service → enquire; contact → directions and visit planning; About → chair rental enquiry. Every service has a relevant WhatsApp enquiry link. The searchable service list gives returning visitors a quick path to what they need.
